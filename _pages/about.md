## Field purpose

Coordinate repeatable shoreline observations for local habitat stewards.

## Visit conditions

Location: East breakwater, Gullhaven Estuary
Season: April through October

## Survey routes

1. TG-14 — Saltmarsh Edge Transect — cordgrass fringe — 90 minutes before low tide — Record wrack depth at every fifth marker.
2. TG-03 — Oyster Bench Loop — intertidal shell reef — low tide to 45 minutes after — Photograph each fixed quadrat without moving shell.
3. TG-27 — Dune Swale Passage — brackish dune swale — sunrise to 09:30 — Log bird detections by sound before visual confirmation.
4. TG-09 — Creek Mouth Drift — tidal creek mouth — first hour of flood tide — Collect floating-litter counts from the marked overlook only.
